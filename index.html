I am currently an Assistant Professor in the Department of Biostatistics and Bioinformatics and an Assistant Director of the Duke Microbiome Center at Duke University. Before joining Duke, I was a visiting assistant professor in the Department of Statistics, University of Wisconsin - Madison from 2018 to 2020, and a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Biostatistics, University of Wisconsin - Madison from 2016 to 2018.

Research Interests

My research focuses on developing statistical methods for the following areas and and providing statistical support for biomedical researchers.

  • Microbiome Research
  • Longitudinal/Temporal Omic Data
  • Integration of Omic Data
  • Spatial Omics
  • High-dimensional Statistical Inference
